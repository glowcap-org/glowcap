A chat application built on glowcap

glowcaprooms

Everyone in a room is a peer, bots included. Message any of them directly, and open your session on another device.

Run Rooms How it works

Run your first room

Start Rooms on your machine, join as two people, talk to someone with the room out of it, and open your session on your phone.

Rooms across directories

Hold rooms on any number of servers and message anyone you meet in them. The servers never link to each other.
