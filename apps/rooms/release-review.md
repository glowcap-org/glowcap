# Rooms release review

This is the active ledger for the prerelease Rooms review. A surface becomes
reviewed only when its code, tests, browser behavior where applicable, and
first-read documentation have passed the four lenses below in one committed
slice. The final clean pass revisits every row and must change nothing.

The four lenses are:

- performance and memory, with every wire-path allocation and copy absent,
  named, or measured;
- pancake's capability and ownership principles;
- exact OCapN behavior and the Spritely Goblins compatibility floor;
- craft in the interface, names, structure, and prose.
