glowcap rooms needs JavaScript

This page is not a view onto a chat server. It runs a peer in your browser: it holds the room as a capability, and messages you send directly to someone travel between the two of you without the room seeing them. None of that can happen without scripting.

glowcap rooms needs a secure address

This page becomes a peer: it holds your room capabilities and direct messages. Over plain network HTTP, somebody on the path could replace it before it becomes yours, and the browser withholds APIs Rooms needs.

Forward this port to your computer and open it at http://localhost, or serve Rooms through HTTPS.
